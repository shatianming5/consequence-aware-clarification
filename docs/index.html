The loop

Decide by running, train by what ran.

01

Write probes

The policy emits at least two pandas readings of the request, each assigning result, plus a stated tag.

02

Execute

A deterministic sandbox runs them on the item’s table. ASK iff the successful outputs diverge.

03

Reward

GRPO pays for a harness verdict that matches the label, and for recovering the reference outcomes. A manufactured divergence scores worse.
